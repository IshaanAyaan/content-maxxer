# Educational reels

The reel workflow converts a grounded note into a narrated 1080×1920 Manim video:

`sources → ordered claims → five-beat script → narration → measured timing → five complementary hand-drawn sketches → captions → loudness-normalized MP4 → actual-frame and transcript QA`

The animation system uses deterministic multi-pass rough strokes, marker lettering, sticky-note callouts, persistent semantic objects, and narration-triggered transformations. Mechanism stories keep the explanatory objects alive across beats—such as coins and probability bars in a Bayesian update—while captions, equations, highlights, and values change on the matching spoken cue. After the opening stage-entry animation, captions and semantic emphasis use separate clocks: the caption phrase appears on its boundary, and emphasis waits for the first aligned semantic word inside that phrase. This keeps the active explanatory object moving without flashing the whole diagram, recoloring mixed groups, or firing a full caption early. Each style has its own motion grammar: hand wobble for whiteboard, drafting-carriage advance for papyrus, pulse plus echo for future minimal, and editorial lift for director cut. Palette, typography, source badge, and caption rail stay coherent across the lesson.

Specialized scripts and artwork are evidence-gated together. The fair-vs-trick-coin Bayes lesson receives its conversational script and coin diagram only when the claims explicitly contain the two hypotheses, likelihoods, normalization values, and posterior. The NVIDIA-versus-Amodei open-weights lesson likewise requires the complete primary-source argument—access, competition, control, irreversibility, defenders versus attackers, rejection of a categorical ban, chip controls, distillation, and testing—before it receives the concise debate script and persistent source map. A TLS 1.3 lesson receives the semantic client/server handshake only when the cited claims establish ClientHello, ServerHello, key shares, derived handshake traffic secrets, certificate verification, Finished messages, application data, and traffic keys. The persistent diagram then advances from hello packets to matched secrets, certificate authentication, transcript checks, and protected data on the matching aligned narration words. Longer otherwise-static spans use sparse labeled packets that follow the active protocol direction; they do not replace or move the aligned word trigger. Incomplete or differently scoped topics never inherit unrelated artwork; they remain on the source-derived causal renderer. A source with at least four beats and a clear ordered mechanism instead receives a reusable process journey. Its cited stages are classified into generic visual operations such as lookup, dispatch, route, transform, verify, resolve, and store. One carrier advances through the stations, completed operations remain visible, and small path transits supply motion during otherwise-static narration. Attention routing requires explicit or combined attention evidence, so a cryptographic `key share` or DNS `query` cannot accidentally summon Q/K/V, softmax, or multi-head artwork.

Argument stories receive the same long-gap discipline as mechanisms, but with a visual grammar that matches debate rather than pretending the source is a process. In the current NVIDIA-versus-Amodei matrix, whiteboard retraces the active hand-drawn evidence path, papyrus gives the active drafting assembly a restrained rotation, future minimal pulses and echoes the active signal, and director cut lifts the relevant editorial layer. One aligned 40.04-second Thalia master was reused without new synthesis. Whiteboard first failed the unchanged motion gate at 20.0%, a stronger cue treatment still failed at 23.5%, and the accepted live-marker treatment reached 30.5%; a visually colliding rewind mark was removed in a final render rather than accepted because QA passed. Papyrus, future minimal, and director cut measured 41.5%, 37.0%, and 42.5% substantial motion. All four retained zero resets, 0.0601-second A/V drift, 1.94% independent-ASR WER, full decode, and clean stable plus motion-selected frames.

That persistent process grammar is implemented in every style, including the default `hand_drawn` path. Hand drawn uses three-pass chalk strokes and wobble emphasis, whiteboard uses marker ink on ruled paper, papyrus uses geared drafting marks, future minimal uses neon rings and pulses, and director cut uses sparse editorial arcs over a cinematic split field. The unseen generic validation uses RFC 1034 DNS resolution and derives `LOOKUP → DISPATCH → ROUTE → RESOLVE → STORE` without any DNS-specific scene code. The generic layout is a spatial journey rather than a flowchart: a low-contrast route establishes the full conceptual space, stage landmarks alternate across the canvas, one carrier moves along a hand-bent segment, and each completed landmark remains available for later narration triggers. The route itself is not an arrow chain or a set of cards.

A controlled spatial-journey matrix reused one byte-identical 91-word Deepgram/Whisper master across hand drawn, whiteboard, warm papyrus, future minimal, and director cut. Their substantial-motion coverage was 45.0%, 41.1%, 63.6%, 62.3%, and 51.0%, respectively. All five retained 0.0201-second A/V drift, 21 valid semantic events, 16 motion witnesses, complete source and layout checks, successful full decode, and clean inspection of both evenly sampled and motion-selected frames. The hand-drawn visual-only A/B increased substantial motion from 44.4% to 45.0% without changing its byte-identical WAV, 2.4-second longest static span, semantic schedule, or synchronization. A style is not approved solely because it renders: the same narration master is used for controlled comparisons, then each encoded MP4 must independently pass motion, semantic timing, layout, audio, synchronization, decode, and representative-frame inspection.

Generic ordered mechanisms receive a separate evidence-preserving editorial pass before synthesis. For every beat, the planner selects a coherent contiguous window of action clauses from that beat's cited claim. The hook and selected evidence are the only permitted narration vocabulary, so tightening cannot invent an analogy, conclusion, or mechanism step. The pass is disabled when a complete specialized script profile applies. In the DNS validation it reduced 121 source words to 91 while retaining all five causal stages. The revised Thalia render at `--voice-rate 190` shortened runtime from 37.96 to 30.08 seconds, moved delivery from 191.3 to 181.5 WPM, improved unprompted Whisper WER from 1.65% to 1.10%, and retained the persistent five-station drawing with 44.4% substantial-motion coverage.

Long ordered sources are no longer truncated to the first five extracted claims. When a compact mechanism contains more than five claims, the planner preserves their order and groups adjacent claims into five bounded beats. A ten-claim U.S. Department of Energy heat-pump lesson therefore retains the contrast with resistance heat plus the evaporator, compressor, condenser, expansion valve, and explicit recurrence. The complete 126-word script remains source-extractive and fits a 43.72-second narration master.

The extractor is tested against subject loss and dangling enumerations. It splits `and` only before a recognizable predicate, keeps noun phrases such as `DOM and CSSOM` intact, rejects connector-led excerpts, and retries with a slightly larger bounded window when the first candidate lacks a subject-bearing action. The last beat can also expand enough to retain the claim's concluding action. A second MDN-grounded browser-navigation source exercises this behavior outside DNS. Its script preserves the DNS lookup, TLS verification, HTTP request, DOM construction, and final paint action in 88 words without ending on a partial resource list.

Visual labels follow the same principle. Sequence markers such as `next` are removed before contextual clauses such as `after the TCP connection` or `as HTML arrives`, so the active landmark foregrounds the narrated action rather than preceding setup. The accepted browser reference therefore displays `TLS verifies`, `the browser sends`, and `the browser tokenizes` on their matching beats. It routes through the generic `LOOKUP → VERIFY → DISPATCH → TRANSFORM → TRANSFORM` journey and does not receive the specialized TLS handshake composition because its source does not contain that complete evidence pattern.

Generic process landmarks receive one more deterministic editorial pass before rendering. The compressor selects a short action and its technical object from the landmark's own cited beat, removes low-information setup language, and may join two complementary clauses with a centered dot. It does not borrow vocabulary from another beat. The resulting label must contain at most eight words, retain at least two normalized source tokens, carry the `source_action_object_compression_v1` record, and contain no ellipsis. The renderer uses a complete, scaled two-line text region rather than the ordinary truncating caption helper. The QA contract checks all five conditions, including the declared render mode, before accepting the reel. Browser and DNS production renders exercise different nouns and operations while passing the same source-overlap and completeness gates.

## Reproduce the three reference renders

Run these from the repository root after installing the `animation` extra and the Cairo/Pango system packages described in the README:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m contentmaxxer director \
  "Why satellites stay in orbit" \
  --job satellites_handdrawn_qwen3_final \
  --output-dir build/handdrawn-reels \
  --source-file examples/reel-sources/orbits.md \
  --hook-style question \
  --renderer manim \
  --voice-provider qwen3 \
  --voice Aiden \
  --voice-rate 165

.venv/bin/python -m contentmaxxer director \
  "How gradient descent learns" \
  --job gradient_descent_handdrawn_qwen3_v2 \
  --output-dir build/handdrawn-reels \
  --source-file examples/reel-sources/gradient-descent.md \
  --hook-style question \
  --renderer manim \
  --voice-provider qwen3 \
  --voice Aiden \
  --voice-rate 165

.venv/bin/python -m contentmaxxer director \
  "How self-attention builds context" \
  --job self_attention_handdrawn_qwen3 \
  --output-dir build/handdrawn-reels \
  --source-file examples/reel-sources/self-attention.md \
  --hook-style question \
  --renderer manim \
  --voice-provider qwen3 \
  --voice Aiden \
  --voice-rate 165
```

Each job contains `video/reel.mp4`, the editable generated Manim scene, the source-backed plan, word timing JSON, SRT captions, actual rendered preview frames, a contact sheet, the original voiceover WAV, citations, a portable manifest, and the complete QA report.

## Narration providers

- `elevenlabs`: premium synthesis with provider character timestamps; requires `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` and a voice ID.
- `deepgram`: explicit opt-in Aura-2 synthesis through the official REST endpoint; defaults to `aura-2-thalia-en`, reads `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` from the environment or `.env.local`, and records response metadata and character counts when returned.
- `qwen3`: local Qwen3-TTS through an external MLX-Audio environment; supports instruction-controlled built-in voices on Apple Silicon. Long beats are generated as bounded sentence/clause chunks to reduce clipped endings, and generation diagnostics are recorded. The tested 0.6B 4-bit model is cached after first use.
- `chatterbox`: MIT-licensed local/open-source synthesis through the official `chatterbox-tts` package; upstream recommends Python 3.11. An optional clean voice reference may be supplied only when you have permission to use that voice.
- `say`: dependency-light local macOS fallback. Audio duration is measured, while word boundaries are distributed proportionally within each spoken beat.
- `file`: imports a finished narration track and apportions it to the grounded script.

`auto` preserves the existing order—configured ElevenLabs, then the external Qwen3 environment, then an installed Chatterbox package, then macOS speech. Deepgram remains explicit opt-in until repeated quality evidence supports changing that behavior. The separate word-aligner default uses a locally cached MLX Whisper model to replace proportional word estimates with cross-attention/DTW timestamps when the transcript maps safely to the exact script. Imported timing maps remain unchanged unless alignment is explicitly requested. Provider, voice, timing method, duration, sample rate, alignment counts, alignment WER and coverage, loudness, peak, silence, and A/V drift are recorded in the job.

Qwen3 does not return provider word timestamps. Beat boundaries still come from measured segment audio; the local aligner can refine the words inside each beat when its acceptance gates pass. Without that model, words remain proportionally distributed. Before publishing, independently transcribe or listen to the rendered voiceover; the checked final set was verified with Whisper large-v3-turbo as described in [OPEN_SOURCE_TTS.md](OPEN_SOURCE_TTS.md).

## Quality contract

Approval requires grounded claim coverage, an explicit explanatory progression, at least four meaningfully different explanatory sketches, concise copy, 25–60 second runtime, readable caption rate, 1080×1920 frames, nonblank and nonduplicate samples, safe layout boxes, audio and video streams, a 48 kHz mono delivery encode, at least 95% script timing coverage, no long dead-air interval, −20 to −12 integrated LUFS, true peak at or below −1 dBFS, at most 0.25 seconds of A/V drift, and a portable complete manifest. An accepted local word alignment adds hard gates of at least 95% timestamp coverage and at most 15% WER. Semantic mechanism, argument, and generic causal reels must also schedule enough valid word-triggered emphasis events for the story; missing, malformed, or out-of-caption events fail QA. A separate non-blocking delivery-cadence signal marks 135–185 WPM as the tested short-form range; the wider 90–220 WPM caption-readability range remains the hard gate so a deliberately slower lesson is not automatically rejected. Those semantic Manim stories also undergo a caption-excluded upper-diagram motion check at 5 fps: at least 25% of sampled intervals must contain substantial motion, with no static run longer than 6 seconds. The signal threshold is recorded in render metadata and is contrast-aware: 0.30 for the standard palettes and 0.18 for thin sepia papyrus strokes. A rejected low-amplitude papyrus prototype still fails at the papyrus threshold, so the adjustment does not turn the gate into a no-op. Each semantic beat must additionally contribute multiple motion-aware witness frames: the strongest interior change, a sustained low-amplitude change, and a distributed peak. A labeled contact sheet also includes the midpoint of the longest static span. Missing files, wrong dimensions, an under-sampled beat, or a missing motion contact sheet fail QA. This witness contract has been exercised on mechanism, argument, and unseen generic-process stories, and on dark chalk and warm-papyrus palettes; a passing unit test alone is not treated as proof of visual quality.

Semantic continuity is independently verified from the encoded MP4. The compiler must declare a semantic-continuity transition contract. The analyzer then samples the settled midpoint of each beat, crops away the headline and caption rail, removes edges shared by every sample as template structure, excludes the intentional hook-to-diagram transition, and measures retained non-template ink from one body beat to the next. Every expected transition must contain at least 60 dynamic edge pixels. A reel passes with at least 0.60 average and 0.65 median retained-edge ratios and no more than one large reset in a standard five-beat lesson. Allowing one reset preserves legitimate semantic morphs such as Bayesian normalization without permitting a sequence of scene replacements. Calibration across accepted DNS, browser, TLS, Bayes, orbit, gradient, attention, and open-weights reels produced averages from 0.6601 to 1.0000; the older gradient scene-replacement baseline measured 0.4592 and fails. A fresh production-path browser render reused the byte-identical accepted narration, passed at 0.8496 average and 0.8464 median retention with zero resets, decoded fully, and passed representative-frame inspection.

Large text swaps are also treated as scene resets. A semantic lesson holds its opening question and unchanged source label for the full reel. Captions begin as source-aligned phrases of at most six words; a trailing one- or two-word orphan may merge back into the previous phrase when the result stays at most eight words and 3.2 seconds. The chalk and whiteboard styles erase the old phrase before writing the next, while the other palettes use their own restrained transition grammar. QA requires no headline replacements, no more than 32 caption transitions per minute, at least 1.7 seconds median caption dwell, and at most 10% rapid technical-trigger phrases. This structural evidence is paired with an encoded title-crop measurement at every beat midpoint: minimum retained title edges must be at least 0.92 and median retention at least 0.95. On the browser control, the old version used 24 caption states, roughly 42 transitions per minute, and 1.31-second median dwell; its changing headlines retained only 0.577 minimum and 0.765 median encoded title edges. The accepted chalk-rewrite version uses 17 caption states, 29.41 transitions per minute, 1.86-second median dwell, and 1.000 minimum and median title retention. It preserved 0.8428 average diagram retention with zero resets, 0.0601-second A/V drift, and the byte-identical narration master. The identical audio and timing map were then rendered through whiteboard, warm papyrus, future minimal, and director cut. All four additional MP4s passed full decode and visual inspection. Encoded title minimum retention was 0.9926 for whiteboard and 1.000 for the other styles; diagram retention stayed between 0.8402 and 0.8641 with zero resets. Motion coverage ranged from a calm 25.2% whiteboard treatment to 50.9% for the drafting-carriage papyrus composition.

Title continuity now includes semantic completeness. Ordinary beat planning may shorten a headline to seven words, but that abbreviated string is no longer reused as a persistent lesson hook. Question hooks are copied exactly from the plan; concise specialized editorial hooks remain unchanged; and long curiosity hooks become bounded topic-derived questions without ellipses. The title renderer balances all words across as many as three lines and scales the complete object to the safe title region. For generic process stories, the duplicate truncated topic inside the canvas is replaced by the first-to-last classified role equation. QA requires a nonempty `persistent_complete_hook`, no ellipsis, an exact plan-hook match for question styles, and matching non-truncated title records in every sampled frame. The accepted browser chalk and papyrus renders each recorded the full `How does a browser turn a URL into a page?` in 8/8 samples. Visual inspection confirmed clean separation from the source label and mechanism canvas; encoded header minimum retention remained 0.9993 and 1.000, respectively.

The ending reuses that same continuous canvas. Generic causal stories declare a `full_route_sweep` recap contract: after the last aligned caption arrives, one highlighted carrier traverses the complete accumulated route from the first classified operation to the final one. The source-derived role pair appears below the route, while the original question remains above it for a natural visual loop. This recap does not rewrite the narration or spend another synthesis request. It is verified from the encoded MP4 rather than from the scene source: a caption-excluding crop is sampled at 10 fps during the final 1.05 seconds, and QA requires at least two active upper-route frames, one active lower-route frame, correct top-to-bottom chronology, and at least 0.2 seconds of traversal. The accepted browser hand-drawn render measured four upper and four lower active frames across 0.7 seconds; warm papyrus measured three and three across 0.6 seconds. Both passed full decode, retained 0.0601-second A/V drift and zero continuity resets, and reused the byte-identical accepted narration WAV.

Feedback-loop classification is deliberately stricter than ordered-process classification. The cited beats must show an expanding action, a corrective action, a later recurrence, and repeated references to the same state. Only then does the compiler declare `source_feedback_loop_v1` and add a visible return path from the final stage to the first; browser-navigation and DNS controls remain `linear_journey`. Encoded recap QA adds lower-stage and upper-left return-frame requirements so a static arc or forward-only sweep cannot pass. The RFC-grounded TCP validation accumulated all five stages on one canvas in hand-drawn chalk, warm inventor-papyrus, and future-minimal signal styles. With byte-identical narration, average retained diagram ink measured 0.8245, 0.8501, and 0.8644 respectively; every style had zero resets, at least four encoded return frames, 0.0401-second A/V drift, clean full decode, and inspected contact sheets.

A separate source-visual profile may enrich that topology, but it has a higher evidence threshold. `tcp_congestion_control_v1` requires all of TCP identity, congestion window, slow start, ACKs, congestion avoidance, retransmission timeout, threshold, and in-flight data. Its persistent state strip shows the window filling, reaching a threshold, shrinking after loss, and reopening under ACKs; landmark and carrier glyphs become packets, gradual growth, loss, and ACK return. The first rendered prototype passed automated QA but was rejected during frame inspection because the state strip was too small and crowded a redundant heading. The accepted third layout promotes the window to the hero diagram and removes the duplicate threshold label. A controlled five-style render then reused the byte-identical 41.96-second Deepgram master. Hand drawn, whiteboard, warm papyrus, future minimal, and director cut measured 29.2%, 27.4%, 51.9%, 46.2%, and 36.8% substantial motion, respectively; every style retained zero resets, 0.0401-second A/V drift, at least five encoded upper-left return frames, full decode, and clean representative frames. The first whiteboard treatment failed the unchanged 25% motion gate at 24.1%; a second landed exactly at 25.0%, and the accepted marker-retrace revision reached 27.4% without weakening QA.

Physical cycles have a separate evidence contract from feedback control. `source_cycle_loop_v1` requires at least four stages, repeated transformations, an explicit return-and-repeat claim, and a shared material or state across the forward and return stages. Without recurrence, the same components remain a linear journey. The heat-pump validation detected refrigerant as the shared state and returned stage five to the evaporator rather than incorrectly treating pressure changes as a TCP-like corrective loop. Its stricter `heat_pump_cycle_v1` artwork additionally requires heat-pump identity, refrigerant, evaporator, compressor, condenser, expansion valve, absorption, release, and recurrence. The accepted whiteboard reel replaces generic gears with distinct absorb, compress, release, and valve-return sketches, keeps a cold/low-pressure to hot/high-pressure state legend, and moves a visible refrigerant token through the route and closing loop.

The first heat-pump encode passed the mechanism and audio checks but failed text cadence at 34.31 caption changes per minute with four one- or two-word orphans. Rebalancing produced 28.82 changes per minute, 1.85-second median dwell, and no rapid captions; that exposed insufficient true diagram motion at 21.5%, which was corrected rather than hidden by lowering QA. The accepted `build/cycle-topology-lab/heat-pump-cycle-whiteboard-v3` encode reached 34.2% substantial motion, a 2.4-second longest static span, 0.8744 average diagram retention, and a verified 2.5-second full-route recap with a 0.6-second lower-to-upper return. The same byte-identical narration and scene contract passed a warm inventor-folio stress test at `build/cycle-topology-lab/heat-pump-cycle-papyrus-v1`: 61.2% motion, 2.0-second longest static span, 0.8869 continuity, zero resets, and a 0.9-second encoded return. The shared Thalia master measured 43.72 seconds, 0% independent-ASR WER with all technical component names recovered, −16.75 LUFS, −2.37 dBFS true peak, zero long dead air, and 0.0801-second A/V drift. Both MP4s decoded fully and both stable and motion-selected frames were inspected.
