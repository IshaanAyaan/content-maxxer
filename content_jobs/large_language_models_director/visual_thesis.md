# Visual Thesis: How Large Language Models Work

## Input

Explain how large language models work: text becomes tokens, tokens become vectors, attention mixes context, transformer layers refine meaning, the model predicts the next token, and repeating that loop creates an answer.

## Thesis

Large language models turn text into tokens, mix context through transformer layers, then predict the next token again and again.

## Central object

A row of token cards becoming vectors, flowing through a transformer, and growing one token at a time.

## Primary transformation

Readable text becomes token vectors, the vectors become context-aware, and prediction becomes a repeated generation loop.
