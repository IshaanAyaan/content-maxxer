# Visual Thesis: Nexus

## Input

Nexus shows why two models can have the same pretraining loss but land in different optimization basins, and why downstream tasks care about the route through the landscape rather than only the score.

## Thesis

Nexus treats pretraining as a path through a landscape, not just a final loss number.

## Central object

Two optimization paths moving across the same loss landscape.

## Primary transformation

Equal final score becomes visibly different landing geometry, then task agreement reshapes the route.
