# Slide Brief: How Large Language Models Work

## Input

Explain how large language models work: text becomes tokens, tokens become vectors, attention mixes context, transformer layers refine meaning, the model predicts the next token, and repeating that loop creates an answer.

## Thesis

Large language models are a repeated next-token prediction loop built on tokens, vectors, attention, and transformer layers.

## Audience action

Swipe away understanding the loop, not memorizing jargon.
