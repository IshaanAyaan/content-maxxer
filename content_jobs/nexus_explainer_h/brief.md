# Brief: Nexus Explainer H

## Source

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.09258

## Goal

Explain the Nexus paper as an intuitive shortform video. The viewer should understand that matching a pretraining score is not enough; the route through the optimization landscape can change downstream behavior.

## Audience

Smart general technical viewers who understand that LLMs train on losses, but may not know optimizer geometry or downstream generalization.

## Constraints

- Target duration: about one minute.
- Visual style: Manim, dark background, clean labels, 3D graph moments.
- Avoid: crowded equations, overlong text, unexplained acronyms.
- Current seed export is visual only. Add narration and captions in a later pass.

## Core takeaway

Optimize agreement, not just the score.
