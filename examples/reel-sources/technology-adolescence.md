# The adolescence of technology

Dario Amodei describes humanity as entering a turbulent technological rite of passage. Humanity may soon be handed enormous power, while it remains unclear whether social, political, and technological systems are mature enough to wield it.

He defines powerful AI as systems exceeding the most capable humans across many fields, acting through digital interfaces, completing long autonomous tasks, and potentially running in millions of copies at much greater than human speed. He summarizes this possibility as a “country of geniuses in a datacenter.”

The essay organizes its major concerns around autonomous systems acting against human intentions, destructive misuse such as biological weapons, the use of AI to seize or entrench political power, and economic disruption that may arrive faster and more broadly than earlier technological shocks.

Amodei argues against both doomerism and complacency. He says uncertainty should be acknowledged, evidence should be sought honestly, defenses should be built, and government intervention should be as targeted and surgically limited as possible.

The essay presents this period as a test humanity can survive, but only by facing the risks directly while deliberately steering powerful AI toward broadly beneficial outcomes.
