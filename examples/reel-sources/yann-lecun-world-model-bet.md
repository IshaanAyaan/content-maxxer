# Yann LeCun's world-model bet

Advanced Machine Intelligence Labs says Yann LeCun is its founding Executive Chairman and announced $1.03 billion in financing in March 2026 to pursue a different path toward advanced machine intelligence; this reel describes that institutional financing—not LeCun's personal money—as a bet against an LLM-only future.

Large language models learn statistical structure in language and generate text by predicting tokens, but LeCun's narrower criticism is not that LLMs are useless; it is that scaling next-token prediction alone is unlikely to produce human-level physical understanding, persistent memory, reasoning, and planning.

AMI Labs says real-world sensor data is continuous, high-dimensional, noisy, and partly unpredictable, so its proposed world models learn abstract representations from video and other sensors, then predict the consequences of actions in that representation space so a system can plan.

AMI summarizes its thesis as “real intelligence does not start in language; it starts in the world,” but the financing makes this a consequential research program, not a demonstrated victory, and world models still have to prove that they can deliver the robust physical understanding and planning their proponents expect.
